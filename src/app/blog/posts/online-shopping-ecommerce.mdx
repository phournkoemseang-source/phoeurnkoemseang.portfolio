## Overview

Online Shopping is an e-commerce platform focused on one of the most important parts of any store: **browsing**. Users can explore product categories, look at what interests them, and get a clean, intuitive shopping experience without friction.

It was also one of my first real collaborative web projects — where I learned how version control, design handoff, and deployment fit together as a workflow.

## The Problem

An e-commerce site lives or dies by how easy it is to browse. The goals were simple but demanding:

- Browse **products by category** effortlessly
- A **clean, user-friendly interface** that doesn't get in the way
- Fast load times and a static, reliable experience

## The Tech Stack

The site is a static website built with **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript** — a deliberately minimal stack that keeps it fast and simple to host.

### Tools That Made It Work

- **GitHub** — version control and collaboration
- **Figma** — design handoff and UI decisions
- **Vercel** — fast, simple deployment

## Key Contributions

- Contributed to **frontend development** with HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript
- Applied version control, design, and deployment tools as a complete workflow

## What I Learned

1. **Browsing UX is the product.** Category navigation and visual hierarchy matter more than flashy features for an e-commerce site.
2. **A simple stack is a feature.** Static HTML/CSS/JS loads instantly, deploys anywhere, and is easy for any teammate to pick up.
3. **The workflow is half the skill.** GitHub, Figma, and Vercel taught me that how a team ships matters as much as what it ships.
